Overview

You are moving from a self-managed PostgreSQL server to Amazon Aurora PostgreSQL using logical replication, keeping cutover downtime to just the final drain-and-switch. Logical replication crosses major versions, so you can upgrade at the same time.

Direction of the connection The Aurora writer (the subscriber) connects outbound to your self-managed server (the publisher) on port 5432. Allow the Aurora instance's address through your firewall / security group and the source's pg_hba.conf. For a private Aurora cluster, connectivity to your data center is over VPN or Direct Connect.
Physical seeds don't apply here Aurora Fast Clone requires an Aurora source. From self-managed, use Replication Copy or a pg_dump seed.
